Category limits

The maximum amount, in USDC, that the agent may approve on its own for each category. Above that, the request is always escalated for human review, no matter how confident the model is.

Decision thresholds

They control when the agent approves on its own, approves a reduced amount, or escalates to a human.

Scoring criteria (Kev / Jev)

The text of each level is what the decision model reads when scoring trust, urgency and importance. Each criterion needs 2 to 10 levels.
